🧩 Light groups

Run different shows on different lights.

Make groupsControl a group

By default all your lights are one wall running one show. Groups let you split them: one show on the curtains, another on the tree, for example.

Make groups

  1. Tap LIGHTS and find Light groups.
  2. Choose All lights, one show (one big wall) or Every light its own show.
  3. In Layout & shape just below, pick One canvas to stretch shows across lights, or Zones to give each light its own area.
  4. Tap Apply & restart lights.

Control a group

With two or more groups, a row of chips appears above the live wall: All groups plus one chip per group. Tap a chip and every button controls only that group. The live preview shows all groups side by side; tap one there to select it.

🧲 Snap to join

The quickest way to make a group: on LIGHTS, drag a light right up against another one and let go. It clicks flush like a magnet and the two shows become one picture. Lights that already share a show get one glowing outline with the show's name.

  • Pull it apart to split it off: drag a light well away from its group and it becomes its own show. Pro
  • Press and hold (or right-click) any light for 🔗 Join, ✂️ Split into its own show and ⇋ Flip it left to right.
  • Every join or split shows a bar with UNDO and ⇋ FLIP IT, in case a light comes out backwards.
  • A string you turned into a curtain or a wall drags from anywhere on its body, just like a curtain.
  • A light laid on top of another on purpose stays in its own show.
Joining or splitting restarts the show for a few seconds. A look you pinned comes back by itself.
