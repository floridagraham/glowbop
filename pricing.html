Pricing questions

Home DJ or Pro DJ?

Home DJ has every look, game, sport, holiday and live feed, the full music show and DJ mode with 12 hit pads. Pro DJ adds the tools working DJs and venues ask for: Autoloops and Scenes, cue lists with a big GO, the tempo toolbox, Song Shows you can program cue by cue, the LIVE BOARD with faders, zones and palettes, override keys, Interlude · Ambient, the Pad Recorder, and MIDI clock and VirtualDJ sync.

Can I try Pro DJ before I pay?

Yes. The live demo is the real console on a pretend wall, and every Pro tool is playable there. Pro DJ monthly and yearly plans also start with a 7-day free trial.

How does the free trial work?

Monthly and yearly plans get 7 days with every feature of that tier. If you cancel before the trial ends, you pay nothing.

What is the Founder deal?

To celebrate the launch, Home DJ Lifetime costs $129 instead of $379 during launch week or for the first 100 buyers, whichever comes first. Same app, every Home DJ feature, every update, forever.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Start monthly and move to yearly or lifetime, or step up from Home DJ to Pro DJ, whenever you like.

Do I need to buy anything else?

Just your lights and a Windows computer. No dongles, sync boxes or extra subscriptions. Song lookup uses free keys.
