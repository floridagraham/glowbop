🧭 Line up & draw your layout

Line up up to 8 curtains, draw your house or stage, and map lights with a camera.

Run the wizardGrid wallsOverlapping curtainsHow shows fitDraw your layoutTemplatesCamera mapping

Lights that aren't curtains, on a house or a stage? Skip to Draw your layout. With more than one curtain (up to 8), GlowBop needs to know three things: which way is up on each one, whether any are mirrored, and what order they hang in. The wizard makes it a one-minute job.

Run the wizard

  1. Tap LIGHTS and, in Line up my curtains, tap Line up my curtains.
  2. Every curtain now shows a big number and an UP arrow.
  3. For each curtain, tap ⟳ to rotate until the arrow points up, and ⇋ if the number reads backwards. Use ⇅ to flip top to bottom.
  4. Use ▲ ▼ to put them in the order they hang, left to right.
  5. Choose how they hang: Side by side, Stacked, Grid or Overlap.
  6. Tap Lock.
Locked means nothing changes by accident. To move curtains later, run the wizard again and lock it when you are done.

Grid walls: up to 8 curtains

Hang curtains in rows and columns, like a video wall: 2 × 2, 3 × 2, 4 × 2 and more, up to 8 curtains.

  1. In the wizard, choose Grid.
  2. Tap the shape your curtains make. The picker shows every rows-by-columns layout that fits your curtains.
  3. Tap each spot and choose which numbered curtain hangs there. A spot can stay empty for an L-shape or a gap.
  4. Check the arrows still point up, then tap Lock.
Every show resizes to the whole grid. Big flowing effects sweep across all of it, and pixel art stays sharp.

Overlapping curtains: a denser wall

Hang two curtains over each other, with the second one shifted so its bulbs sit in the gaps of the first. Mixing brands works too, like a small Twinkly centered inside a bigger Bluetooth curtain. Same space, twice the bulbs, and GlowBop draws the picture twice as sharp to match.

→

Sideways

Shifted half a gap left or right. Rows stay level, columns double up.

↓

Down

Shifted half a row down. Columns stay lined up, rows double up.

↘

Diagonal

Shifted half a gap both ways, so every bulb sits in the middle of four others.

  1. In the wizard, choose Overlapping. The first curtain in the list is the base.
  2. Under each other curtain, pick its Place: 🎯 Center (a smaller curtain sits in the middle of the bigger one) or Top-left.
  3. Then pick its Shift ½: Sideways, Down or Diagonal, to match how you hung it.
  4. Tap 🔒 Lock it in.
Bulbs not quite in the gaps? Curtains from different brands can space their bulbs differently, and twisted wires can turn bulbs. Run camera mapping after hanging: it records exactly where every bulb landed.
You can also set it per curtain: in Your lights on the LIGHTS tab, change a curtain's Overlap to "Over" another curtain and pick the shift.

How shows fit your wall

GlowBop builds one big canvas from all your curtains. Flowing effects stretch across the whole wall. Pixel-art scenes (pumpkins, characters, the tree) keep their sharp look by showing one copy per curtain instead of getting blurry.

  • 1 curtain: everything as designed.
  • Side by side: wide shows sweep across, pixel art repeats per curtain.
  • Stacked: a taller canvas, great for fire, rain and fountains.
  • Grid: a big video-wall canvas, rows and columns together.
  • 3D shapes: see 3D shapes.

🧵 Lay out your lights on the live wall

  1. Tap LIGHTS. The live wall at the top is the editor: every curtain and string shows where it hangs.
  2. Drag a curtain to move it. Drag a string by its START or END. Press and hold (or right-click) for Add a bend here. The real bulb under your finger blinks white.
  3. Use 🧲 Snap to grid to click onto bulb spots, or Free to place freely. ↶ Undo and ↷ Redo (or Ctrl+Z) step back and forward.
  4. On an iPad, pinch to zoom and slide with two fingers. Tap ✏️ Customize full screen for a bigger stage.
  5. Tap Save. The show plays across all your lights as one picture.

👆 Which light is which?

  • Tap to identify: on the layout screen, tap a light's name. The real light flashes for a moment.
  • Identify all: under Your lights, tap Identify all. Every light flashes in turn and its name pops up on screen.
  • Use its Twinkly app map: if you mapped a Twinkly in the Twinkly app, tap Use its Twinkly app map on that light and then Yes, use it. The lights restart for a moment with the new layout. A Twinkly that was never mapped there tells you so.
  • Layout history: tap Layout history to see your last 5 layouts. Tap one to put it back. The layout you had a moment ago is kept too, so you can always switch back.

🤖 Bluetooth curtains (Surplife)

  1. Close the Surplife app on your phone (the curtain takes one connection at a time), then Find my lights.
  2. Tap the curtain on the live wall. Its controls show at the top.
  3. Autopilot (the default) plays the curtain's own effects, picked by the music. Builds and drops switch to Shapes.
  4. Under Built-in effects, tap ◀ ▶ to try one on the curtain for 20 seconds. Tap Star it on the ones you like: Autopilot plays your stars.
Wi-Fi lights getting choppy while the curtain runs? Many laptops share one radio for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. A USB Bluetooth adapter gives the curtain its own.

💾 Lights on their own

  • Laptop-off show: let a song play for a minute, then on LIGHTS tap Save the last minute. GlowBop saves a short piece of the show into each Twinkly. When GlowBop stops, they keep playing it. Preview 20 s shows them now; Turn it off hands the lights back to the Twinkly app.
  • Full Twinkly? GlowBop asks before replacing what is saved on it.
  • Follow its own effects: on a light's card, set Follow to Its own effects. It plays its built-in effects, a new one every part of the song.
  • When the music stops: choose GlowBop calm look or Each light's own effect.
  • Max brightness: on a light's card, pick 25, 50, 75 or 100 percent.

🎞️ Nanoleaf: live pixels or smooth loops

  • On a Nanoleaf string's card, Show on it picks how it plays: Live pixels (the default) or Smooth loops.
  • Live pixels: every bulb is part of the picture, like a curtain. It keeps up with the music, even on busy Wi-Fi.
  • Smooth loops: one loop per part of the song in the show's colours, a flash on every drop. Best on busy Wi-Fi.
  • Can't keep up? GlowBop switches a struggling string to Smooth loops by itself, then tries Live pixels again later.
Done for the night? Tap SHUTDOWN at the bottom of the rail and pick Lights off.

🪢 Make a string into a curtain

  1. Pick the string, then Make it a curtain.
  2. Choose the height: Match main, 11, 16, 21, or any height with − and +.
  3. GlowBop works out the drops (308 bulbs become 15 drops of 21) and shows a hanging guide: start at the top beside your other lights, run the first drop down, the next one back up, and keep snaking. Spare spots at the end stay dark.
  4. Main panel: tap ⭐ on the curtain or string wall that leads. Every other light matches its spacing, so text and shows line up across all of them.
Adding a new string light? Setup asks if you want to make it a curtain that matches your main panel.

✏️ Draw your layout

Open LIGHTS → Draw your layout and tap Open editor. Photograph your house or stage, or start from a blank grid, and draw where each light string runs. Use lines, paths, arcs, rings, boxes and pixel grids, or one-tap templates.

  1. Pick the light under Your lights, then a tool, and draw the string from where it starts, in wiring order.
  2. Set how many bulbs are on that part. Bulbs tucked behind a gutter? Hide them so they take no room on the path.
  3. Tap Test: the first bulb of each string blinks green, the last blinks red, then a white light runs from start to end, so you can check the direction.
  4. Name each string (Roofline, Front window, Big tree). Every named string becomes a zone for Pro DJ.
  5. Tap Save. The show restarts with your real shapes.

Drop one on your photo, then drag the corners to fit. Effects then follow your real shapes: spirals climb trees, light runs down from the roof peaks, arches pulse from the top, and windows light up in turn on the beat. Eight shape-aware effects join the show by themselves.

📷 Camera mapping at night

Map at night with the phone on a tripod. In the editor tap Map outdoors (or LIGHTS → Map with camera for a single string). GlowBop guides you and makes sure it found every bulb. Then line the pieces up in the editor.

Tips: wait until it is dark and turn off porch lights. Put the phone on a tripod, a car roof or a fence post: it must not move. On iPhone, tap and hold on the lights in the Camera app first to lock the exposure.
