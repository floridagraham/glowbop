How do I get my lights in team colors?

This part is easy with almost any smart light. Most brand apps let you pick colors and save them as a scene. A few tips so it looks right:

  • Pick two colors, not five. Team colors read best as a strong main color and one accent.
  • Skip the white if you can. On many lights, white washes out the other color. Use it as the accent, not half the wall.
  • Check the shade. Navy, maroon and dark green can look dim on LEDs. Bump them a touch brighter than you think.
  • Save it as a scene. Then game day is one tap, every week.

Can my lights react when my team scores?

This is the harder part. A scene sits still. To celebrate a touchdown or a goal, the lights need to know the score as it happens, which means an app that follows live games. That is less common, and some people end up building their own with score feeds and code. If you just want it to work on game day, look for an app that has live scores built in.

Tip. Put the lights where the room can see them and the TV, not behind the TV. The celebration is half the fun when everyone sees it go off.

How do I set up lights at a tailgate?

  • Power first. Battery-powered curtains make it easy. Otherwise, plan your power source before you plan the show.
  • Bring your own network. A phone hotspot puts your laptop and lights on one network with no stadium Wi-Fi needed.
  • Shade the screen. A canopy helps the lights pop before sunset.
  • Tie it down. Curtains catch wind. Clip the bottom so it does not swing.
