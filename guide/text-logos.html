🔤 Text, logos & mirror

Messages, logos and mirroring your screen.

TextLogoSaved itemsScreen mirror

Text on the wall

  1. Open Text & logos and type your message.
  2. Pick a Mode: Scroll (marquee), Beat words (one word per beat), Still, or On drop (shows on each drop).
  3. Pick a Color: Look (follows the show), Rainbow or White.
  4. Pick a Layer: Over the show or Text only. Set the speed, then tap Show text.
Flash a word on every drop: type one big word like the birthday kid's name or "JUMP" and it hits the wall each time the beat drops.

Your logo

  1. Under Logo, tap Choose a picture. A PNG with a see-through or black background works best.
  2. Pick a Motion: Glow, Pulse or Colour shift.
  3. Tap Show logo, or Text + logo for both.

Great for DJs, bars, gyms and businesses. Save it and it's ready next time.

Saved messages and logos

  • Tap Save under the text or the logo to keep it. It appears as a chip at the bottom of the card.
  • Tap a saved chip to put it back on the wall.
  • To delete one, tap the ✕ next to it. It turns red and asks Delete?. Tap again to delete.
  • 🗑️ Remove logo from the wall clears the picture that's loaded right now (your saved copy stays).

Screen mirror

Show the laptop screen on the lights: a game, a movie, a music video.

  • Style: TV (the picture), Beat (the picture pulsing to the music), Glow (soft ambient color).
  • Area: Smart fill trims black bars so the video fills the wall; Center zoom shows bigger shapes.
Mirror works best with the video playing full screen on the laptop.
