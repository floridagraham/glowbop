🎃 Holidays & mini-movies

Holiday buttons and the animated stories.

HolidaysMini-moviesChoose effects

Holidays have their own section so they don't pop up in the middle of a July pool party. Tap a holiday and the wall plays only that holiday until you stop it.

The holidays

Mini-movies

For the two biggest nights, GlowBop plays a short animated story in chapters, drawn as crisp pixel art made for LED curtains.

Choose the effects

Inside each holiday you can toggle which effects are in the rotation, so you can keep the ones your family loves.

Trick-or-treat tip: start Halloween at dusk, set Pace to Chill, and the mini-movie plays every few switches between the spooky effects.
