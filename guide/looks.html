What a look is

A look is a mood: a color palette plus a set of effects for calm, medium and loud moments. During a song GlowBop picks effects from the current look, and moves to a new look at drops or when the song changes.

3D, club, nature and retro looks

  • 3D Depth: tunnels, spinning cubes, disco spheres and black holes that look like they go deep into the wall.
  • Club Rig: moving-head beams, laser scans and strobe banks, like a real club ceiling.
  • Dance Crew: pixel characters that dance to the beat. Turn them on or off with Characters.
  • Retro Arcade: self-playing pixel games: brick breaker, snake, pong, alien parades and 8-bit fireworks.
  • Wild Elements: rain on the window, a volcano that erupts on the drop, a tornado, a waterfall and the surface of the sun.

Quick scenes and Describe a scene

Quick scenes are one-tap presets. Describe a scene lets you type what you want ("slow purple ocean with gold sparkles") and tap Play it. GlowBop picks matching effects and colors from your words.

Feature an effect

Love one effect? Feature it for the current part of the song, or Pin it to keep it on. Tap Let the show flow again to go back to normal.

Transitions

Choose how one effect hands over to the next: quick cuts for high-energy sets, long fades for a chill night.
