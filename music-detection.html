What it hears

Ten things your lights finally understand

💥

Builds and drops

It feels a build winding up and lands the drop with the wall: a big move, a new look, right on the hit. Not every loud moment, just the real ones.

🥁

The beat and bar one

It locks onto the tempo and finds the first beat of the bar, so changes land where a DJ would put them, not halfway through a phrase.

📖

Every part of the song

Intro, verse, chorus, build, drop, breakdown, outro. Each part gets its own feel, and a chorus that comes back gets a fresh twist.

🎬

The one real climax

Movie music swells for minutes and peaks once. GlowBop grows the wall through the crescendo and saves the big moment for the true peak.

🧠

Song memory

The first time a song plays, GlowBop learns its shape. Next time it already knows where the drops and the climax are, and sets up for them.

🎸

What kind of music it is

Rock, hip-hop, Latin, country, jazz, kids songs, soundtracks, Christmas songs: each gets its own looks automatically, and you can correct it once per artist.

🌗

The mood

Bright and happy or dark and moody, the colours follow how the song feels.

🔀

When the song changes

A new song gets a new look on its own, whether it comes from a playlist, YouTube or your DJ software.

🔊

Any app, any speaker

Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, DJ software or a video: it hears what your computer plays. Bluetooth speaker delay is fixed with one slider.

🎛️

You can take over

Hold Charge the drop to build it yourself, or tap the beat in. When you let go, GlowBop picks the song back up.
