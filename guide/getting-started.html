🚀 Getting started

Install, find your lights on the LIGHTS tab, connect your phone.

What you needSet up your lightsInstallFind lightsPhone & iPadPress play

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 laptop or PC. This is where GlowBop runs and listens to the music.
  • One or more supported LED curtains (see Supported lights).
  • Your home Wi-Fi. The lights, the computer and your phone all join the same network. (Bluetooth lights just need to be near the computer.)
  • A phone (iPhone or Android) for the remote. Optional, but it's the fun part.

1. Set up your lights

Set up each light with its maker's app first (for Twinkly, the Twinkly app). Once it shows up there and lights up, you're ready. GlowBop takes over from here.

Tip: use your main Wi-Fi, not a guest network. Guest networks often block devices from seeing each other.
Nanoleaf: in the Nanoleaf app, connect the light to Wi-Fi (not just Bluetooth), then open its settings and turn on the API ("Connect to API"). GlowBop can only see a Nanoleaf once its API is on. Then tap Find my lights and tap the Nanoleaf to pair it (once).

2. Install and open GlowBop

  1. Download GlowBop and run GlowBop-Setup. It installs just for your Windows account, no admin password needed.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. (Your browser may also say the file "isn't commonly downloaded": choose Keep.) This shows up for new apps from small makers, not because anything is wrong.
  3. When it finishes, leave Open GlowBop now ticked and click Finish. Next time, open GlowBop from the Start menu or desktop shortcut. The start-up screen shows each light as it is found; tap Start now to begin with the ones already there.
  4. Paste the license key from your Polar email (it starts with GLOWBOP- for Home DJ or GBPRO- for Pro DJ) and tap Unlock.
  5. If Windows asks, allow GlowBop on private networks. That lets your phone and lights reach it.

3. Find your lights

  1. Tap LIGHTS on the side rail, in the System group at the bottom. In Your lights, tap Find my lights. GlowBop searches your Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
  2. Each light it finds is added with its size. If one is missing, use Add by brand.
  3. Have more than one curtain? Do the line-up wizard next. It takes a minute.

Everything about your lights lives on LIGHTS: Your lights, Light groups, Layout & shape, Draw your layout, Line up my curtains and Map with camera. The Lights key under the live wall jumps straight there.

Bluetooth lights (TikTok-style pixel curtains, DayBetter strips) take one connection at a time. Close their phone app before you tap Find my lights.

4. Open the console on your phone or iPad

  1. Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
  2. On the laptop, tap SETUP and find Control from your phone. Point your phone camera at the QR code.
  3. The console opens in Safari or Chrome. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen to get the GlowBop icon. From then on it opens full screen, like an app.
  4. On an iPad, turn it sideways for the big two-column board.
  5. GlowBop off on the laptop? Open it on your phone or iPad anyway and tap Start GlowBop. The laptop starts it for you, as long as it is open and awake.
  6. Want a friend to run the lights too? On a connected phone the same card is called Share the remote: they scan the code right off your screen, or you send the link.
Not connecting? Same Wi-Fi on both, VPN off on the phone, and allow GlowBop on private networks in Windows. More in Troubleshooting.

5. Press play

Play a song on the computer, from any app. The bar under the live wall says MUSIC SHOW · LISTENING and the wall comes alive. That's it, you're running a light show. 🎉

Next, take two minutes with Using the console to learn the rail and the keys.
