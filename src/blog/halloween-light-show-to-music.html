What do I need for a simple Halloween light show?

On Halloween night you do not need a yard full of props to wow the street. A beginner show can be four things:

  • One LED curtain or a few string lights. A curtain is best, because it can show pictures like pumpkins and bats.
  • A speaker near the front door.
  • A computer inside to run the show, on your home Wi-Fi.
  • Your phone as the remote, so you can change things from the porch.

Where should I hang the lights?

Pick the spot trick-or-treaters walk toward.

  1. A covered porch. Hang the curtain behind the porch posts or across the back wall. It stays dry and frames the door.
  2. Or a front window. Hang the curtain inside, facing out. No weather worries at all, and it works great in apartments and townhouses too.
  3. Hang it flat and straight. A curtain that sways or bunches up bends every picture. A tension rod or a few hooks along the top help.
  4. Keep the controller box reachable. You may need to unplug and replug it, so do not bury it behind the bushes.
  5. Stay close to the router. Many smart lights only use 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. The closer the curtain is to your router, the smoother the show.
Tip. Outside means outdoor-rated. Check that your lights are rated for outdoor use, use outdoor extension cords, and keep plugs up off wet ground.

How do I set up the speaker and music?

  • Put the speaker by the door, pointed at the walkway. It does not need to be loud. Kids only need to hear it from the sidewalk.
  • Be kind to the neighbors. Turn it down after trick-or-treat hours.
  • A wired speaker is the safest bet. Bluetooth works, but it plays a moment late. If you use one, line the lights up with a sync setting. More in why are my lights behind the beat.
  • Build a playlist ahead of time: a mix of spooky classics and party songs, plus a few calm ones for when the street is quiet.

When should I test it?

The night before, at dusk. Daylight hides problems. Walk out to the sidewalk and look back at the house. Is the picture the right way up? Can you hear the music from the street? Does your phone reach the computer from the porch? Fix it now, not with a line of tiny ghosts waiting.
