🎃 Holidays & mini-movies

Holidays for your country and the animated stories.

Your countryHolidaysWorldwideHoliday songsMini-moviesChoose effects

Holidays have their own section, HOLIDAY, so they don't pop up in the middle of a July pool party. Tap a holiday and the wall plays only that holiday until you stop it.

Holidays for your country

GlowBop shows the holidays of the country you live in, with the right dates and local names, worked out on your computer. It picks the country from your location on the LIVE tab, or choose one at the top of HOLIDAY. More holidays from other countries shows the rest of the 23. When one of your holidays is on or within 10 days, the HOLIDAY key moves up the rail next to LOOKS.

Holiday songs

Play a Christmas song from mid-November to early January, or a Halloween song in October, and the music show switches to that holiday's show by itself. Out of season, the song gets the normal show.

Mini-movies

For the four biggest nights, GlowBop plays a short animated story in chapters, drawn as crisp pixel art. They're wide-screen: on 2, 3 or 4 curtains the scene stretches across the whole wall (the witch flies the full length, the sleigh crosses every curtain).

  • 🎃 Halloween Night: fog, a graveyard, a witch across the moon, haunted houses, bats, ghosts, screaming jack-o'-lanterns on the drop, a lightning storm.
  • 🦃 Harvest Day: sunset on the farm, falling leaves, a strutting turkey, the harvest moon, dinner by candlelight, a whirlwind of leaves on the drop.
  • 🎄 Christmas Eve: snow on the village, windows lighting up, a giant tree filling with ornaments, Santa's sleigh, presents bursting into fireworks.
  • 🎆 New Year Countdown: the city skyline, confetti, a 10-to-1 countdown as the ball drops, fireworks at midnight, a champagne toast.
🎄 Got a Christmas tree? Wrap any string of lights round it and it becomes part of the show: 22 tree animations, your colours and star, on the wall with your curtains or a show of its own. See Christmas tree mode →

Choose the effects

Inside each holiday you can toggle which effects are in the rotation, so you can keep the ones your family loves.

Trick-or-treat tip: start Halloween at dusk, set Pace to Chill, and the mini-movie plays every few switches between the spooky effects.
