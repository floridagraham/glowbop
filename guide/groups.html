🧩 Light groups

Run different shows on different lights.

Make groupsControl a group

By default all your lights are one wall running one show. Groups let you split them: one show on the curtains, another on the tree, for example.

Make groups

  1. Open Light setup → 🧩 Light groups.
  2. Choose All together (one big wall) or Each light separate.
  3. Pick a Layout: One canvas stretches shows across lights, Zones gives each light its own area.
  4. Tap Apply & restart lights.

Control a group

With two or more groups, a row of chips appears on the remote: ✨ All groups plus one chip per group. Tap a chip and every button controls only that group. The live preview shows all groups side by side; tap one there to select it.
