FAQ

Your questions, answered

Can't find yours? Email hello@glowbop.com.

Do you sell the lights?

No. GlowBop is software: the controller for LED lights you already own (Twinkly curtains today, more on the way). Ditch the app that came in the box and take total control of your lights, from your phone, iPad or laptop.

What is GlowBop?

A Windows app that turns LED curtain lights into a live light show. It listens to your music and also does holiday scenes, scoreboards, text, logos and screen mirroring. You control it from your phone.

Home DJ or Pro DJ: what's the difference?

Home DJ is the whole light show: the music show, DJ mode with hit pads and skip keys, every look, game, holiday, fire style, sport and live feed, text and logos, and up to 8 curtains. Pro DJ adds the tools for gigs and venues: Autoloops and Scenes, cue lists, the tempo toolbox, Song Shows, the LIVE BOARD with zones and palettes, override keys, Interlude · Ambient, the Pad Recorder and MIDI clock and VirtualDJ sync. See Pricing.

Can I upgrade to Pro DJ later?

Yes. Start with Home DJ and step up whenever you like. Paste your Pro DJ key in SETUP, under License, and the Pro tools unlock right away. Nothing to reinstall, and your lights and settings stay as they are.

Does Pro DJ need different lights or hardware?

No. Pro DJ lighting for the lights you already own: it runs on the same Windows computer and the same lights as Home DJ. A DJ controller is optional on both plans. It does not run DMX moving heads or fog machines. See Pro DJ.

Does it work with Serato, rekordbox or VirtualDJ?

Yes. GlowBop listens to the audio the laptop plays, so it works with any DJ software or music app, no plugin needed. With Pro DJ, VirtualDJ (over OS2L) and MIDI clock give the tightest sync. There is no Ableton Link, Pioneer Pro DJ Link or Denon StageLinQ yet. See GlowBop Pro DJ vs DJ lighting software.

Is the try demo the real app?

Yes. glowbop.com/try is the real GlowBop console running on a pretend light wall in your browser. Every page works, including DJ mode, and the Pro DJ tools are playable there too, so you can fire Autoloops, run a cue list, build a Song Show or ride the faders before you buy.

Which lights does it work with?

Twinkly curtains, Nanoleaf string lights and the LIFX String today. TikTok-style pixel curtains, DayBetter strips and WLED are built in and being tested, and light-only Christmas trees are coming. Govee works as a one-color wash only. See Supported lights.

Do I need a Mac version?

GlowBop runs on Windows 10 and 11 for now. Want Mac? Email us. Requests decide what we build next.

What music apps does it work with?

All of them. GlowBop listens to the sound your computer plays: Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Amazon Music, DJ software, videos, anything.

Can I play music from my phone?

GlowBop listens on the computer, so play the music on the computer and send it to your speakers. Bluetooth speakers work; use the Sync slider to line them up.

I only have string lights. Does it work?

Yes. Open LIGHTS and the live wall is the editor: drag your string to where it really hangs, add bends, or tap Make it a curtain. Pick a height (match your main panel, 11, 16, 21 or any) and GlowBop works out the drops, for example 308 bulbs become 15 drops of 21, and shows you how to hang it. Twinkly and Nanoleaf string lights can sit right next to your curtains as one wall. See how.

What is Pro Art?

Pro Art (BETA) puts your lights in any shape: hearts, letters, stars or your logo. Map a light from a drawing (SVG, PNG or JPG), a photo of the lights switched on, a spreadsheet (CSV), an xLights .xmodel or a GlowBop sign file (.glowbop), and share your whole wall as a .glowbop file. It is free for everyone until Oct 31 (Halloween), then part of Pro. See Pro Art.

Can my Christmas tree join the light show on my wall?

Yes. Set up your tree on the HOLIDAY tab, then on the LIGHTS page drag it next to a curtain. It stands there at its real height and dances as part of the show. Flip the switch to Own show and it plays tree animations on its own, still to the music.

Does it work with xLights?

It is in the works for the 2026 holidays. Point GlowBop at your xLights show folder and it reads your props and controllers (Falcon, Kulp, HinksPix, ESPixelStick, WLED, Falcon Player) and plays live, music-reactive shows on your house, with no sequencing. One tap hands the house back to your own schedule. xLights is a free, separate program; GlowBop is not made by or affiliated with the xLights team.

Does it need a microphone or a dongle?

No. It listens to the computer's own sound, so room noise and talking don't throw it off.

Do I need to install an app on my phone?

No. Scan the QR code and the remote opens in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen for an app icon.

Can it just be a fireplace?

Yes. Tap 🔥 Light the fire and the whole wall becomes flickering flames, no fireplace frame or logs. Pick from 9 styles (campfire, candles, torches, lava, dragon breath and more), 9 colors and Cozy or Roaring. See the Fireplace guide.

Can I use it outside or at a game?

Yes. At home, use your Wi-Fi. At a field, connect the laptop to your phone's hotspot. Battery-powered curtains make this easy.

How many curtains can I connect?

Up to 8 as one wall: side by side, stacked, overlapping or a grid of rows and columns. You can also split lights into groups that each run their own show. It all lives on the LIGHTS tab.

Will it slow down my computer?

GlowBop is light. A normal laptop runs the show and music at the same time. Plug in for long parties.

Is my data private?

GlowBop runs on your computer. It doesn't record or upload your music. Song lookup only sends the song title and artist to look up genre and tempo.
