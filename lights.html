Supported lights

Will it work with your lights?

GlowBop doesn't sell lights. It's the software that takes control of the ones you have. Here is exactly where things stand. We only mark a light "Supported" after we have run it ourselves.

Your lights

Supported

Twinkly curtains

Twinkly Wi-Fi curtains, up to 8 in a row, a stack or a grid, or wrapped into 3D shapes. Tested on our own 2-curtain wall. Twinkly string lights join in too: lay them out on the live wall or turn them into a curtain.

Supported

Nanoleaf string lights

Nanoleaf string lights on your Wi-Fi, found by GlowBop and paired once. Hang them beside your Twinkly curtains and they play one picture together: lay them out on the live wall, turn them into a curtain, or shape them into a logo with Pro Art.

Built in · testing

TikTok-style pixel curtains

The low-cost Bluetooth RGB pixel curtains sold on TikTok Shop (Surplife app). Found over Bluetooth. Our test panels are on the way.

New · testing

DayBetter strips

DayBetter Bluetooth strip lights and light poles. Each strip glows one color at a time, picked from the show and pulsing with the beat.

Built in · testing

WLED

DIY strips and matrices running WLED, found on your Wi-Fi.

Supported

LIFX String lights

We bought and tested the LIFX String. Rainbows and chases flow smoothly down the whole string. Set it up in the LIFX app, then GlowBop finds it on your Wi-Fi. LIFX Neon, Beam and Lightstrip talk the same way but are not tested yet.

Color wash only

Govee

We bought and tested the Govee Curtain Lights Pro. In GlowBop it works as one color for the whole curtain. It makes a nice color wash beside your wall, but it can't show scores, words or pictures. For a real pixel wall, pick Twinkly or Nanoleaf.

In the works · holidays 2026

xLights houses

Already run a holiday display with xLights? Point GlowBop at your show folder and it reads your props and controllers (Falcon, Kulp, HinksPix, ESPixelStick, FPP) and plays live, music-reactive shows on your whole house. Your mega tree, arches and roofline become zones. One tap hands it back to your schedule.

Coming soon

Light-only Christmas trees

Metal-frame cone trees made of addressable lights (Hello Fairy app).

Good to know

Curtains are the sweet spot

  • Curtains are a grid of pixels, so GlowBop can draw real pictures: pumpkins, scores, words.
  • More curtains = a bigger screen. Two side by side is a great start.
  • Outdoor-rated curtains handle pool decks and porches.
