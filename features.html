Music show

Hears the music, follows the song

GlowBop listens to the sound your computer is playing, so it works with any app: Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Serato, a playlist on a USB stick.

  • Beat, bass and melody each drive different parts of the show.
  • Build-ups and drops get their own moves.
  • Pace Chill, Normal or Hype, and Sparkles Off, Auto or Lots.
  • Sync slider lines the lights up with Bluetooth speakers that run late.
  • Song memory remembers how each song went the next time it plays.

DJ mode

Play the lights like a club lighting tech

DJ has its own key on the rail. The music show keeps running, and you add moves on top, by hand, right on the beat.

  • Hit pads: Drop, Strobe, Flash, Fire burst, Confetti, all landing on the next beat. Blackout holds the wall dark.
  • Hold to build: the wall winds up brighter and faster; let go and it drops.
  • Cue the next look to come in on the next drop.
  • Tap tempo when the room is too loud, and crowd lines like HANDS UP in big letters.
  • DJ controller: plug a USB pad controller into the laptop and teach it every pad.

Big Wave

Waves that roll across the wall

A new family of flowing effects with its own look. A giant wave crests on the beat, a serpent swims the length of your wall with a glowing head, twin waves cross and an ocean swell rolls in slow.

  • Big Wave, Serpent, Twin Waves and Ocean Swell.
  • Their own Big Wave look, and they show up in Molten, Neon Party and Storm & Sea too.
  • Smooth on one curtain, spectacular across eight.

Looks

34 looks, 200+ effects

A look is a mood: its colors plus a set of effects for calm, medium and loud parts. GlowBop shuffles through them as the music changes.

  • Neon Party, Deep Space, Synthwave, Warehouse Rave, Mainstage, Club Rig, Royal Gold and more.
  • 3D Depth tunnels, spinning cubes and disco spheres.
  • Dance Crew pixel characters you can switch on or off.
  • Describe a scene in words and GlowBop builds it.
  • Pin an effect you love, or hit Surprise me.

Games

Games that play themselves

GAMES has its own key on the rail. Tap a game and it plays itself across your wall, speeding up with the music. Or start the Arcade marathon and GlowBop plays them all in turn.

  • 8 games: Breakout, Snake, Pong, Alien parade, Space rocks, Block drop, Light cycles and Game of life.
  • Breakout: bricks burst into sparks. Snake: a rainbow snake hunts glowing food.
  • Pong: the ball smashes on the beat.
  • ♫ Back to music under the wall ends the game.

3D shapes

Bend your lights into art

Wrap your curtains around a cylinder, a box or a globe. GlowBop knows the shape, blends the seam so the picture goes all the way round, and plays effects made for 3D.

  • Cylinder, box or globe, with an optional lid curtain on top.
  • 3D effects: Spinning Globe, Barber Spin, Rising Rings and Bounce Ball in the 3D Object look.
  • A 3D preview in the app you can drag to spin.
3D shapes guide →
Fireplace

Nine kinds of fire

Light a fire on your wall with one tap. GlowBop simulates real flames live, so the fire never loops and never looks the same twice.

  • 9 styles: Fireplace, Campfire, Bonfire, Candle wall, Torch row, Pixel flames, Spirit fire, Lava rising and Dragon breath.
  • 9 colors: Classic, Blue gas, Purple, Rainbow, Green, Pink, Ice, Team colors (your two colors) and Night shift (slowly drifts through the colors all night).
  • Cozy or Roaring, and Dance so the flames swell with your music.
  • Flare on the beat: every kick sends the flames up, and loud parts blow gusts of wind through them.

Holidays

Every holiday gets its own button

Holidays live in their own section, away from the normal shuffle. Tap one and the wall plays only that holiday until you stop it.

  • Halloween, Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Year, Valentine's, St. Patrick's, Birthday and Game Day.
  • Wide-screen mini-movies: Halloween Night, Harvest Day, Christmas Eve and New Year Countdown.
  • Jack-o'-lanterns, bats, ghosts, a haunted house, sleigh rides, fireworks, a ball drop and more.
  • Choose which holiday effects are in the rotation.
Holidays guide →
Live feeds

The outside world, live on your lights

Tap LIVE and pick a feed. It takes over the wall until you turn it off. Set your ZIP code or city once for weather, storms and what's overhead.

  • Countdown to anything, with giant seconds at the end and a fireworks finale.
  • Weather: big temperature over an animated pixel sky, today's high and low, the next hours.
  • Storm watch: flashes new National Weather Service warnings over the music, only if you turn it on. Tap Stop any time.
  • Overhead now: planes flying over your house and the space station.
Live feeds guide →
Scoreboard

Game Day, Live Fan and F1

Turn the wall into a scoreboard. Keep score yourself from your phone, follow a real game, or watch a Grand Prix.

  • Game Day: 15 sports, clock that counts down or up, periods, timeouts, fouls, possession, undo.
  • Live Fan: pick a league and team; scores update every 10 seconds and the wall celebrates in team colors.
  • Live stats: the count, runners on base, batter and pitcher, the last play scrolling by, down and distance, plus a Game Center on your phone.
  • F1: running order, tyres, pit stops, fastest lap, DRS and rain.
  • Countdown timer: a huge clock for halftime, a party or New Year.
Scoreboard guide →
Formula 1

Race day on your wall

The F1 board shows the top of the field with team colors and tyre dots, then celebrates a new leader, pole position or a fastest lap.

  • Purple bar for the fastest lap, just like TV.
  • Pit stops get a soft flash, not a party.
  • Works for practice, qualifying and the race.

Epic mode

Made for movie music

Cinematic songs don't have a "drop". They swell for minutes and peak once. GlowBop notices and switches to Epic mode on its own.

  • A slow, growing swell through the crescendo.
  • One big climax at the real peak, not every loud moment.
  • No harsh strobes during the quiet, emotional parts.
  • Optional song lookup uses free genre data to spot soundtracks even faster.

The console

A light-show console in your pocket

GlowBop has a brand-new control panel. A colored side rail jumps between sections, the live wall shows exactly what your lights are doing, and big keys sized for thumbs run the show.

  • Nine sections on the rail: Music, Looks, Scenes, Holiday, Fire, Sports, Live, Text and Setup.
  • Live wall mirrors your lights in real time. Tap Full view for the whole screen.
  • iPad sideways becomes a two-column board: wall on the left, controls on the right.
  • Console sounds with a mute key, a screen-stays-on switch, and a friendly screen if the laptop goes out of reach.

Multi-curtain

Up to 8 curtains, one wall

Hang curtains side by side, stack them, or build a grid of rows and columns. GlowBop treats them as one big screen and resizes every show to fit, keeping pixel art crisp.

  • Grid walls: tap a shape like 2 × 2, 3 × 2 or 4 × 2 for up to 8 curtains.
  • A line-up wizard shows a number and arrow on each curtain so you can rotate, flip and order them.
  • Lock it in so nothing moves by accident.
  • Run them as one show or split them into groups with their own shows.
Line-up guide →
Phone remote

Control it all from your phone

The GlowBop laptop app shows a QR code. Scan it on any phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi and the full console opens in the browser.

  • Live preview of every curtain right on your phone.
  • Big keys sized for thumbs on every section.
  • Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with its own icon.
  • Works at the field too, over your phone's hotspot.
