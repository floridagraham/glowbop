🛠️ Troubleshooting

Fixes for the common snags.

My phone says "Your lights are taking a breather"

That screen means your phone can't reach the GlowBop laptop right now. Check the three things it lists: the same Wi-Fi, the laptop awake, and GlowBop open on the laptop. It reconnects by itself as soon as the laptop answers, or tap Try again now.

The console won't open on my phone

  • Phone and computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. Not the guest network, and not cellular.
  • Turn off any VPN on the phone.
  • In Windows, allow GlowBop on private networks (Windows Security → Firewall → Allow an app).
  • Make sure your Wi-Fi is set to Private in Windows, not Public.

GlowBop can't find my lights

  • Check the curtain shows up and works in its own app first.
  • Restart the curtain (unplug for 10 seconds) and tap Find my lights again on the LIGHTS tab.
  • Use Add by brand if it still hides.
  • Nanoleaf: it must be on Wi-Fi (not only Bluetooth) and its API must be turned on in the Nanoleaf app (light settings → Connect to API). If pairing times out, tap Connect to API again and tap the Nanoleaf in GlowBop within 30 seconds.
  • Bluetooth lights: close the light's own phone app (they take one connection at a time), keep them within about 30 feet of the computer, and make sure Bluetooth is on in Windows.

The lights don't react to music

  • The music must play on the computer GlowBop runs on. Check the sound is actually coming out (volume up, right speaker selected).
  • The mode bar should say MUSIC SHOW · LISTENING. If something else has taken over (a holiday, the fire, a live feed), tap ♫ Back to music.

The lights are early or late

Use the Sync slider. Bluetooth speakers usually need it slid right. See Sync.

A curtain is upside down or out of order

Run the line-up wizard again, fix it, and lock it in.

🩺 Check my lights

On LIGHTS, tap Check my lights. In about five seconds GlowBop asks every light how its Wi-Fi is doing and tells you, in plain words, what is wrong. Most answers come with a one-tap fix.

  • Offline or slowed down lights, with the brand and the reason.
  • Weak Wi-Fi for one light: move it (or its controller box) closer to the router, or add a Wi-Fi extender near it.
  • Several lights dropping at the same moment means the router, not the lights. On your router's settings page set the 2.4 GHz channel to Manual on 1, 6 or 11 and the channel width to 20 MHz.
  • Curtains split into separate shows or overlapping on the map: one tap puts them together or lines them up.

"A light is struggling" heads up

Every brand gets the same heads up: which light, which brand and why. GlowBop gives that one light a breather by itself while the rest of the wall keeps going at full speed.

📶 Light health

On LIGHTS, the Light health panel scores every light from 0 to 100 and says why. The same score shows as five little bars after each light's name on the live wall, and the whole setup's score sits in the top bar.

  • Missed check-ins or slow to answer on a Wi-Fi light: try Run the Wi-Fi test and Scan Wi-Fi neighbours, then set your router's 2.4 GHz channel to the one it suggests.
  • Bluetooth signal weak or reconnected on a Bluetooth strip: move the strip or the computer closer together, and close the strip's own phone app.
  • Your computer is playing its sound over Bluetooth: Bluetooth speakers and headphones share the computer's radio with Wi-Fi, so the lights answer slower and miss check-ins. Connect the speaker with a cable, or play the music from another device.

Lights flicker or stutter

  • Move the router closer, or the curtain's controller closer to the router.
  • Close heavy programs on the laptop and plug it into power.

My phone keeps going to the lock screen

Tap the console once after it opens: the screen then stays on. Check SETUP → Screen says Screen stays on.

Something is stuck or acting up

Open SETUP → Something not working? and tap 🔄 Restart GlowBop twice. It restarts the laptop app from your phone in about 10 seconds and your song picks up where it left off. 📱 Reload this screen refreshes just the phone or iPad.

The wall is stuck dark, white or strobing

  • Look at the mode bar. If ⛔ OVERRIDE ACTIVE shows, tap it to release the override keys. A 🎚️ FADERS chip means a fader is away from normal: tap it, then ↺ Reset all.
  • On the DJ page, the ⬛ Blackout pad holds the wall dark until you tap it again. Check Power at the top of the page too.
  • Pro DJ: a cue blackout or an Autoloop holds the wall. ♫ Back to music lets them all go.
  • In an interlude, tap 🎵 Back to the show. Anything else: ♫ Back to music.

My Song Show didn't play Pro DJ

A show only plays when GlowBop knows where in the song it is. Play the song on GlowBop's own player or a music app that shares its timeline with Windows, or start the song from the beginning. Check Song Shows is on and Play this song's show is on for that song. The SHOWS page tells you why when it can't play. See How it follows the song.

Live scores aren't updating

Scores only change while a game is live. Before kickoff the board shows the start time. Check the computer is online.

Still stuck? Tap Help & feedback at the bottom of the console to send us a report, or email hello@glowbop.com with what you see and your light brand.
