Holiday buttons and the animated stories.

Mini-movies

For the four biggest nights, GlowBop plays a short animated story in chapters, drawn as crisp pixel art. They're wide-screen: on 2, 3 or 4 curtains the scene stretches across the whole wall (the witch flies the full length, the sleigh crosses every curtain).

  • 🎃 Halloween Night: fog, a graveyard, a witch across the moon, haunted houses, bats, ghosts, screaming jack-o'-lanterns on the drop, a lightning storm.
  • 🦃 Harvest Day: sunset on the farm, falling leaves, a strutting turkey, the harvest moon, dinner by candlelight, a whirlwind of leaves on the drop.
  • 🎄 Christmas Eve: snow on the village, windows lighting up, a giant tree filling with ornaments, Santa's sleigh, presents bursting into fireworks.
  • 🎆 New Year Countdown: the city skyline, confetti, a 10-to-1 countdown as the ball drops, fireworks at midnight, a champagne toast.

Choose the effects

Inside each holiday you can toggle which effects are in the rotation, so you can keep the ones your family loves.

Trick-or-treat tip: start Halloween at dusk, set Pace to Chill, and the mini-movie plays every few switches between the spooky effects.
