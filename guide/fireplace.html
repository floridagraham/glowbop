Guide

🔥 Fireplace

Nine fire styles, nine colors, cozy or roaring, flare on the beat.

Light itStylesColorsCozy / RoaringWith musicBig walls

Turn the whole wall into a fire with one tap. There's no fireplace frame and no logs, just the flames, simulated live so they never loop.

Light the fire

  1. Tap FIRE on the side rail.
  2. Tap Light the fire. The mode bar turns orange and says FIREPLACE 🔥.
  3. To stop, tap Put out fire, or ♫ Back to music under the live wall from any section.

9 fire styles

  • 🔥 Fireplace: the classic: tall flames with a white-hot core and floating embers.
  • 🏕️ Campfire: crossed logs, glowing coals and a cone of flame. Wide walls get two.
  • 🎇 Bonfire: a huge blaze that roars up on the drop.
  • 🕯️ Candle wall: rows of candles, each flickering on its own.
  • 🔦 Torch row: torches on a brick wall lit by their own flicker.
  • 👾 Pixel flames: chunky 8-bit cartoon fire.
  • 👻 Spirit fire: slow, ghostly blue flames with curling wisps.
  • 🌋 Lava rising: molten lava with popping bubbles, rising and falling.
  • 🐉 Dragon breath: embers, then a jet of fire blasts across the wall.

9 colors

🔥 Classic 💙 Blue gas 💜 Purple 🌈 Rainbow 💚 Green 🩷 Pink 🤍 Ice 🏟️ Team colors 🌙 Night shift

Team colors lets you pick any two colors (great for game day). Night shift slowly drifts from classic to purple, blue and green over the night.

Rainbow drifts slowly through every color across the wall and keeps a white-hot core. Green is great for Halloween and St. Patrick's, Pink for Valentine's, Ice for a ghostly winter look.

Cozy or Roaring

  • 🛋️ Cozy: tall, relaxing flames that reach up the wall, with a small flare now and then.
  • 🔥 Roaring: fast, wild flicker with big whooshes of flame every few seconds and bursts of sparks.

Calm, Dance or Flare on the beat

  • 🕯️ Calm: the fire ignores the music. Perfect for a quiet night.
  • 💃 Dance: the flames swell gently with the beat. Fire that grooves.
  • Flare on the beat: every kick sends the flames up with a burst of sparks, and when the music is loud, gusts of wind sweep through the fire.

On a big wall

With several curtains, GlowBop builds one long bed of fire across all of them, like a modern linear fireplace. Stacked curtains get taller flames.

Good to know: the fireplace stays lit until you put it out. The fire, the scoreboard and live feeds take turns: turning one on turns the others off. Text and logos still show on top of the flames.
