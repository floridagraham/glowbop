Guide

🌐 3D shapes

Wrap curtains around a cylinder, a box or a globe.

Pick the shape3D previewSeamless wrap3D effects

Curtains bend. Wrap them around a big tube, a box or a ball and you have a glowing art piece. Tell GlowBop the shape and it wraps the picture all the way around, with no seam where the ends meet.

Pick the shape

  1. Tap LIGHTS. In Layout & shape, find Shape.
  2. Choose Flat wall, Cylinder, Box or Globe.
  3. For a cylinder or box you can pick a Curtain on top (lid). That curtain is drawn as rings that close the top.
🥫

Cylinder

Curtains wrapped around a tube or column. The picture loops around forever.

📦

Box

Curtains around the four sides of a cube or pillar, with an optional lid.

🌍

Globe

Curtains around a ball. Effects use latitude and longitude, like a planet.

The 3D preview

As soon as you pick a shape, the live wall turns into a 3D model of your piece, lit with the real show. Drag it to spin it around and look at every side.

The seamless wrap

Where the last column meets the first, GlowBop blends a narrow band so effects flow straight across the join. No hard line, no jump.

3D effects

The 3D Object look has effects made for wrapped lights. They know your shape, so a globe spins like a planet and rings rise around a column.

The clips are shown unrolled, flat. On the real piece they wrap around. Every other look works on a shape too.

Build tip: use something light and sturdy, like a pool noodle frame, a lampshade wire or a big plastic ball. Keep the curtain controller at the back.
