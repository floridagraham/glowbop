🎨 Looks, effects & games

What a look is

A look is a mood: a color palette plus a set of effects for calm, medium and loud moments. During a song GlowBop picks effects from the current look, and moves to a new look at drops or when the song changes.

Tap LOOKS, then any look to jump to it. Turn on Pin to keep it, or tap Release in the mode bar to hand control back to GlowBop.

🌙 Calm pack: soft when the song is soft

Not every song wants strobes. Movie scores, chill songs and jazz stay calm and flowing, with 12 calm effects (aurora veils, cathedral rays, sky lanterns, piano keys, bowed strings, moonlit snow and more) and four calm looks: Cinematic, Concert Hall, Jazz Club and Moonlit.

GlowBop picks this by itself. To tell it, use the Jazz, Chill or Epic light style on MUSIC. See Light styles.

🎮 Sprites at play

The glowing sprites play games with each other, in time with the music. The Sprite Party look runs eight of them in cyan, magenta and yellow: Leapfrog, Follow the leader, Jump rope, Limbo, Ring around, Musical chairs, Bubble pop and Tug of war. Toy Box adds Tag, Hot potato and Hide and seek. More sprites join in on bigger walls.

🛋️ Chill Lounge and Deep Calm

Two calm looks with six slow effects that drift instead of flash: Color clouds, Breath waves, Slow ripples, Glow trails, Velvet melt and Two tone. They change with soft melt, drift, dip and glow fade transitions. Chill Lounge is warm and cool (peach, rose, lavender, teal), Deep Calm is blues and violets.

🌀 Fractal Dreams

Fractal Dreams draws real fractals live: Mandelbrot dive, Julia dance, Fractal tree, Carpet zoom and Newton bloom. Flowing, melodic songs pick it by themselves.

🎸 Genre looks

When song lookup knows the genre, Auto gives each kind of music its own look, with no extra buttons:

  • Rock: Rock Arena. Hip-hop: Velvet Bounce. Latin and Afrobeats: Tropicana.
  • Country and folk: Golden Porch. Kids songs: Toy Box.
  • Christmas and Halloween songs switch to the holiday show, in season.

The MUSIC page shows the guess, like Auto · Rock.

🌊 Flow pack

Melodic bass, progressive and chillstep get the Flow style with two looks, Trip and Silk, and 14 flowy effects: silk and ribbons, liquid and ink, waves and tides, and particle flocks like a murmuration of birds. Each one has a dreamy mood for the verses and a bigger drop mood when the song lands.

🎛️ VJ Lab

Ten visuals in the style VJs use: Chladni sand patterns, Pixel melt drips, Morphogenesis coral, Laminar flow, Time rift, Particle field, Light sweep, Outline racers, Blinds and Spatial expansion. Pick the VJ Lab look, or meet a few of them inside Psychedelic, Trip, Synthwave, Neon Party and Warehouse Rave.

👁️ Eyeball and spin

Now and then a giant eye peeks in, looks around and blinks. Don't want it? Turn off Eyeball in Show control on MUSIC. Spinning effects now turn both left and right, so the wall feels less repetitive.

3D, club, nature and retro looks

  • 3D Depth: tunnels, spinning cubes, disco spheres and black holes that look like they go deep into the wall.
  • Club Rig: moving-head beams, laser scans and strobe banks, like a real club ceiling.
  • Dance Crew: pixel characters that dance to the beat. Turn them on or off with Characters.
  • Retro Arcade: the self-playing pixel games now live under their own GAMES key (below).
  • Wild Elements: rain on the window, a volcano that erupts on the drop, a tornado, a waterfall and the surface of the sun.

Big Wave 🌊

The newest family of effects, with its own Big Wave look. They also turn up in Molten, Neon Party and Storm & Sea.

  • Big Wave: a giant glowing wave that rolls and crests on the beat.
  • Serpent: a smooth snake with a glowing head that swims the length of your wall.
  • Twin Waves: two waves crossing, with sparkles on the crest.
  • Ocean Swell: a slow, deep swell for calm songs.

See the looks and the newest effects in the gallery →

Arcade games 🕹️

The self-playing games have their own key, GAMES. Tap a game and it plays on the wall, speeding up with the music:

  • Breakout, Snake, Pong, Alien parade, Space rocks, Block drop, Light cycles and Game of life.
  • 🕹️ Arcade marathon plays every game in turn.
  • Tap the playing game (or the marathon) again to turn it off. ♫ Back to music under the wall ends it too.

Quick scenes and Describe a scene

Under SCENES (below the fireplace), Quick scenes are one-tap presets. Describe a scene lets you type what you want ("slow purple ocean with gold sparkles") and tap Play it. GlowBop picks matching effects and colors from your words.

Feature an effect

Love one effect? Feature it for the current part of the song, or Pin it to keep it on. Tap Let the show flow again to go back to normal.

Transitions

Choose how one effect hands over to the next: quick cuts for high-energy sets, long fades for a chill night.
