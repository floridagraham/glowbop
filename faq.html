FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Email hello@glowbop.com.

What is GlowBop?

A Windows app that turns LED curtain lights into a live light show. It listens to your music and also does holiday scenes, scoreboards, text, logos and screen mirroring. You control it from your phone.

Which lights does it work with?

Twinkly curtains today. TikTok-style pixel curtains, light-only Christmas trees, WLED and Govee are being tested. See Supported lights.

Do I need a Mac version?

GlowBop runs on Windows 10 and 11 for now. Want Mac? Email us. Requests decide what we build next.

What music apps does it work with?

All of them. GlowBop listens to the sound your computer plays: Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Amazon Music, DJ software, videos, anything.

Can I play music from my phone?

GlowBop listens on the computer, so play the music on the computer and send it to your speakers. Bluetooth speakers work; use the Sync slider to line them up.

Does it need a microphone or a dongle?

No. It listens to the computer's own sound, so room noise and talking don't throw it off.

Do I need to install an app on my phone?

No. Scan the QR code and the remote opens in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen for an app icon.

Can it just be a fireplace?

Yes. Tap 🔥 Light the fire and the whole wall becomes flickering flames, no fireplace frame or logs. Pick from 7 colors and Cozy or Roaring. See the Fireplace guide.

Can I use it outside or at a game?

Yes. At home, use your Wi-Fi. At a field, connect the laptop to your phone's hotspot. Battery-powered curtains make this easy.

How many curtains can I connect?

Up to 4 as one wall, side by side or stacked. You can also split lights into groups that each run their own show.

Will it slow down my computer?

GlowBop is light. A normal laptop runs the show and music at the same time. Plug in for long parties.

Is my data private?

GlowBop runs on your computer. It doesn't record or upload your music. Song lookup only sends the song title and artist to look up genre and tempo.

When does it launch?

Halloween 2026. Join the launch list and we'll email you.
