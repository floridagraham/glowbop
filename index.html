One console, every screen

Scan a QR code and the console opens in your browser. The colored rail is grouped: Perform for music and DJ, Visuals for looks, scenes and fire, live feeds, sports and more, Pro for the Pro DJ tools, System for your lights and settings. How it works

From box to light show in 5 minutes

No DMX, no light programming, no extra music gadgets. If music plays on your computer, GlowBop can see it.

Hang your lights

Hang your curtains and connect them with the maker's app, like you normally would.

Open GlowBop

It finds your lights on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, then a quick line-up wizard tells it which way is up and which hangs where.

Press play

Play any song from Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or a DJ app. The wall starts dancing. Grab the console on your phone.

New · DJ mode

Play the lights like a club lighting tech

DJ has its own key. The music show keeps running and you add moves on top, by hand. Pads fire the moment you touch them, or turn on Snap to beat and every hit lands on the beat.

  • 12 hit pads: drop, strobe, flash, fire burst, confetti, blackout, sweep, shockwave, color bomb, lightning, 3-2-1 and heartbeat.
  • Skip keys: back or forward one look or one effect, with the names shown right below.
  • Hold to build, let go to drop, in 7 build styles: Rise, Close in, Burst out, Spin, Rainbow, Zipper and Heartbeat, or Auto for a new one each time.
  • Cue the next look, tap tempo and big crowd lines like HANDS UP.
  • Plug in a DJ controller and teach it every pad, or pick a ready-made layout for Akai and Novation pads.
  • Pro DJ adds Autoloops, cue lists and a LIVE BOARD with faders and the Pad Recorder.

Smarter than "sound reactive"

It knows a drop when it hears one

Most light apps flash on every beat. GlowBop follows the whole song.

  • Build-ups fill the wall slowly, getting brighter and busier.
  • Drops hit with a burst, a new effect and a color change.
  • Quiet parts go soft and flowing so the room can breathe.
  • Epic songs get one big climax instead of strobes.
