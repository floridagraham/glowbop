⚖️ Home DJ vs Pro

What every plan includes, what Pro adds, and what Pro DJ and Pro Home do.

Side by sidePro DJPro HomeTry Pro

Every GlowBop plan is the full app. Home DJ already runs a live light show that hears every beat, build-up and drop, with all the looks, holidays, sports, live feeds, text and games. Pro is for bigger setups and people who perform: unlimited lights, the full DJ toolkit, and two extra modes, Pro DJ (a lighting desk for gigs) and Pro Home (run the whole house). Upgrading is just a new key; nothing to reinstall.

Pro DJ mode, in one line

Tap PRO DJ on the rail and the DJ page turns into a lighting desk: the music player, faders, loop banks and cues on one page, 6 assignable Pro keys under the live wall, and a layout you can arrange around your rig. More in Pro DJ tools.

Pro Home, in one line

Tap PRO HOME on the rail for the house control center: one-tap house scenes (Welcome home, Dinner, Party, Movie night, Game day, Relax, Bedtime, All off), schedules that turn the lights on at sunset and off at bedtime, zones and the holiday calendar. More in Pro Home.

Try Pro before you buy

On Home DJ the Pro keys stay in full colour with a small 🔒 PRO badge. Tap one and GlowBop shows what it does. The live demo has every Pro tool switched on.

Already on Home DJ? Buy Pro, then paste the new key in SETUP › License. Your looks, layouts and settings all stay.
