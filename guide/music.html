🔊 Music show

How the show follows a song, Epic mode, sync and song lookup.

How the music show works

GlowBop listens to the sound coming out of your computer, the same sound you hear. It doesn't matter what app plays it: Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, a DJ program, a video. Nothing to log into.

  • Every part of the music drives a different part of the show.
  • Melody moves the flowing parts.
  • Song sections: GlowBop knows a verse from a build-up, a drop or a breakdown.
  • Drops get a big move and usually a look change.

Show controls

Everything here lives under MUSIC, in Show control and Levels.

  • Pace: Chill slow and dreamy, Normal, Hype fast changes for a dance floor.
  • Sparkles: Off, Auto (on the high notes), Lots.
  • Brightness for the whole wall, and Blend for how smoothly effects cross-fade.
  • Surprise me throws in a random look and effect. Characters turns the pixel dancers on or off.
  • Next effect skips ahead. Pin keeps the current one going. Shuffle lets GlowBop choose.

Sync with Bluetooth speakers

Bluetooth speakers play sound about 150 to 300 milliseconds late, so the lights can look early. Slide Sync delay to the right until a kick drum and its flash land together. GlowBop remembers the setting for each speaker.

Quick test: play a song with a hard, simple kick drum and watch one curtain while you slide. When it feels "locked", stop.

Epic mode 🎬

Movie and trailer music works differently from dance music: a long crescendo, then one huge peak. GlowBop spots these songs on its own and switches to Epic mode.

  • Through the build, the wall swells: more light, more motion, warmer color.
  • Only the real climax gets the big moment.
  • Harsh strobes are left out of Epic songs.

Light styles: Auto, Epic, Dance, Chill, Jazz

Under Now Playing on MUSIC, Light style shows what GlowBop thinks the song is. Auto shows its guess, like Auto · Rock: rock, hip-hop, Latin and Afrobeats, country and folk, kids songs and flowing melodic music each get their own looks, and epic, chill and jazz songs stay calm. A Christmas or Halloween song switches to the holiday show, in season.

Not right? Tap Epic, Dance, Chill or Jazz and GlowBop remembers it for that artist from now on. Tap Auto to let it decide again. See calm and genre looks.

Song memory

The first time a song plays, GlowBop learns its shape. Next time, it already knows where the drops and the climax are and can set up for them.

Song lookup (optional)

When GlowBop knows a song's name, it can look up its genre, tempo and key. Soundtracks start in Epic mode right away. You need two free keys:

  1. Last.fm (genres): sign in at last.fm, go to last.fm/api/account/create, fill in a name and description, and copy the API key.
  2. GetSongBPM (tempo and key): request a key at getsongbpm.com/api and click the activation link in your email.
  3. In GlowBop tap SETUP → Song lookup, paste each key, and tap Save.
Each song is looked up only once and saved on your computer. Your keys stay on your computer too.
