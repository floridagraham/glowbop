Get GlowBop for Windows

Version 1.1.0 · Windows 10 and 11 · about 24 MB. Installs just for your Windows account, no admin password needed.

⬇️ Download GlowBop-Setup.exe

You'll need a license key to unlock it. Start a 7-day free trial to get one.

Install in 4 steps

  1. Open the downloaded GlowBop-Setup.exe. If your browser says it "isn't commonly downloaded", choose Keep.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for new apps from small makers, not because anything is wrong.
  3. Click Install, then Finish with "Open GlowBop now" ticked.
  4. Paste the license key from your Polar email (it starts with GLOWBOP- for Home DJ or GBPRO- for Pro DJ) and tap Unlock. Then follow the setup guide to find your lights.
Moving to a new computer? On the old one, open SETUP → License and tap Move my license to another computer, then install here and paste your key.
For the careful: check the file

SHA-256 of GlowBop-Setup.exe:
323b3a7bdfc489c957072ca86da2946104ffdaa1371b4d310351129422a8f5e1

How do I uninstall?

Windows Settings, then Apps, then GlowBop, then Uninstall. Your lights go back to their own apps.
