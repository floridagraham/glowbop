Autoloops · LOOPS & CUES

32 beat-synced looks, ready to play

Four banks of eight: Club, Chill / Flow, Big Room and Color, each pad a look, one or two effects and a palette. Tap a pad and it drops in on the next bar and holds the wall, or let Advance move on every 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars.

  • Next bar or Snap now, with a fade you pick (cut to 4 s).
  • Each pad at ¼, ½, 1, 2 or 4 times the beat.
  • A fifth bank holds your Scenes.

Scenes · LOOPS & CUES

Save the moment, bring it back

Love how the wall looks right now? Save it as a Scene: the look, the effect, the colours and the faders. Recall it later with a smooth fade, from a pad, a cue or your controller.

  • Up to 64 scenes, renamed and reordered any time.
  • Use them in cue lists for your big moments.

Cue lists with GO · LOOPS & CUES

Run a wedding with one button

Plan the event top to bottom: Grand Entrance, First Dance, Cake, Last Song. Then tap the big GO for each moment. Each cue crossfades in over its own fade time.

  • Cues can be an Autoloop, a Scene, a Blackout, an Interlude or Back to Auto, each with a note.
  • Now and Next in big letters, Back, jump to any cue.
  • A list per event. On a laptop, the space bar is GO.

Tempo toolbox · LOOPS & CUES

Every flash on the kick

Fine-tune the BPM in 0.1 steps, double or halve it, nudge the beat earlier or later and lock it. Auto Ambient goes calm by itself when the music stops, and comes back with it.

  • ±0.1 and ±1 BPM, x2 and /2, Early and Late.
  • Auto Ambient after 5, 10, 20 or 45 seconds of silence.

Program a whole song, once

  • Plays the same way every time that song comes on.

Pro DJ questions

Do I need a controller?

No. Everything works by touch on a phone, iPad or the laptop. A MIDI controller is a bonus: plug it into the laptop, pick a ready layout or long-press any control to teach it a button.

Does it work with Serato, rekordbox or Traktor?

Yes. GlowBop listens to the audio your laptop plays, so it works with any DJ software or music app, with no plugin. For the tightest beat, use VirtualDJ (GlowBop follows its beat over OS2L) or send MIDI clock from your software. There is no Ableton Link, Pioneer Pro DJ Link or Denon StageLinQ yet: those need licence or partner deals.

Does it run DMX moving heads or fog machines?

No. GlowBop is made for consumer LED lights: Twinkly curtains today, with WLED, Govee and Bluetooth curtains built in and in testing. If your rig is DMX stage fixtures, a DMX app like SoundSwitch or Lightkey is the better fit.

Are the shows pre-mapped to the song?

The auto show reacts live: it hears the build, the drop and the break as the song plays, and remembers songs it has heard. Want a fixed show? Program it once in Song Shows, or let Auto-draft write one.

How do I set up VirtualDJ?

In VirtualDJ open Settings, then Options, search for os2l and set os2lDirectIp to 127.0.0.1:9996. Restart VirtualDJ, then pick VirtualDJ under Beat from in GlowBop's Controllers & Sync.

My controller isn't listed. Now what?

Long-press any control to teach it a button, and use Test my controller in SETUP: press pads and move faders, then tap Send to GlowBop support. The test comes straight to us so we can add your controller.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The live demo is the real console on a pretend wall, and every Pro tool is playable. Pro DJ monthly and yearly plans also start with a 7-day free trial.
