Guide

📡 Live feeds

Countdowns, weather, storm watch and what's overhead.

LocationCountdownWeatherStorm watchOverhead

Live feeds bring the outside world onto your lights. Tap LIVE, then a big key to put that feed on the wall. Tap it again, or ♫ Back to music under the wall, to go back to the music show.

Set your location once

Weather, storm watch and overhead need to know where you are. In LIVE → Live feeds, type a ZIP code or City, State and tap Save. GlowBop shows the place it found underneath.

🎉 Countdown

  • Pick a ready-made countdown: New Year's, Christmas, Halloween or the 4th of July. They always point at the next one.
  • Or add your own: a name, a date and time, and a finale message like HAPPY BIRTHDAY.
  • Choose a color. The wall shows days, then hours, minutes and seconds. The last minute is giant pulsing seconds, then fireworks and your message.
  • Preview the finale shows the ending right now, so you know what to expect.

🌦️ Weather

A big temperature over an animated pixel sky: sun and rays, clouds, rain, thunderstorms, snow, fog, and the moon and stars at night. It flips between Now, Today (high and low) and Next hours. Pick °F, °C or Auto. It checks for new weather every 10 minutes.

🌀 Storm watch

Storm watch is different: it runs quietly on top of the music show, and only if you turn it on. When the National Weather Service puts out a new warning, watch or advisory for your spot, the wall flashes and the alert name scrolls by. A warning keeps a thin red edge glowing until it ends. During hurricane season it also lists named storms and how far away they are.

  • Test alert shows what an alert looks like. While it's on the wall the key turns into Stop the alert, and ♫ Back to music under the wall clears it too.
Not an official warning system. Always follow NWS alerts, your phone's emergency alerts and local officials. Alerts work for US locations.

✈️ Overhead now

A radar of the planes flying over your house right now, with their direction, plus the International Space Station when it comes by. Choose how far out to look. When the space station is as close as it gets, the wall celebrates. Wave hi! 👋
