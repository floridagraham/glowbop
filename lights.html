Good to know

Curtains are the sweet spot

  • Curtains are a grid of pixels, so GlowBop can draw real pictures: pumpkins, scores, words.
  • More curtains = a bigger screen. Two side by side is a great start.
  • Outdoor-rated curtains handle pool decks and porches.
