✏️ Arrange your screens

Move and resize the boxes on any page. Each screen keeps its own layout.

StartMove & resizeCompact & ResetEvery screen

Every page of the console can be laid out your way. Move the boxes you use most to the top, make the big buttons bigger, and push the rest out of the way. GlowBop remembers it.

Start arranging

  1. Open the page you want to change (MUSIC, DJ, LOOKS, any of them).
  2. Tap ARRANGE on the left rail, right under SETUP.
  3. A bar appears at the top of the screen, and every box gets a dashed outline and a handle with its name.

Move and resize

  • Move: drag a box by its name handle. The other boxes slide out of the way and everything stays packed with no gaps.
  • Resize: drag the bottom-right corner of a box. Make CHARGE THE DROP huge if you like; the button grows with its box.
  • Done: tap Done on the top bar. Your layout is saved.

Compact and Reset

  • ⚡ Compact lays the page out tight for you, every box sized to what's in it. A great starting point; then drag to fine-tune.
  • Reset goes back to GlowBop's own layout for that page.

Every screen keeps its own layout

A laptop, an iPad held sideways, an iPad upright, an iPhone upright and an iPhone sideways each get their own layout, because what fits on one doesn't fit on another. Arrange once on each and GlowBop switches by itself when you turn the device. Layouts are kept on the GlowBop computer, so a second iPad picks up the same arrangement.

Pro pages (Shows, Loops & Cues, Live Board, Override, Pro DJ and Pro Home) can be arranged once Pro is unlocked. See Home DJ vs Pro.
