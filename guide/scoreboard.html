Guide

🏟️ Sports

Game Day, Live Fan in 44 leagues, F1, golf, racing, tennis, UFC and the countdown clock.

Game DayLive FanLive statsF1Golf, racing, tennis, UFCCountdown

Tap SPORTS on the side rail and pick a mode: Game Day, Live Fan or F1. Golf, racing, tennis and UFC are in the Live Fan league list. Off returns to the light show.

Game Day: keep score yourself

  1. Choose a Sport from the dropdown: Soccer, Football, Basketball, Baseball, Softball, Hockey, Field hockey, Lacrosse, Volleyball, Pickleball, Tennis, Rugby, Aussie rules, Cricket, Water polo, Cornhole, Other, plus a Countdown timer.
  2. Open ⚙️ Game setup to set minutes per period, number of periods, and ⏳ Count down or ⏱️ Count up. Tap Set up the game.
  3. Tap ▶ Start to run the clock. Nudge it with −1 min −10 s +10 s +1 min.
  4. Add points with the score buttons, or type the score straight into the box. −1 fixes a mistake.

Timeouts, fouls and the ball

  • Use − and + for timeouts and fouls. They show as dots on the wall.
  • Tap the ball button to switch possession.
  • Baseball and softball track balls, strikes and outs. Football tracks down and distance.
  • Use the arrows to change period, quarter or half.

Fix-it buttons

  • ↶ Undo takes back the last change (up to 40 steps).
  • ⇄ Swap sides flips the teams at halftime.
  • New game clears everything (it asks first).

Every score gets a celebration on the wall, and it doesn't always say "GOAL". Expect SCORE, TOUCHDOWN, a big burst of color and more.

At the field: bring the laptop, turn on your phone hotspot, connect the laptop to it, then open the remote link on your phone. Battery-powered curtains make this easy.

Live Fan: follow a real game

  1. Tap Live Fan, pick a League, then your Team. There are 44 leagues in five groups:
    • 🇺🇸 Pro: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, MLS, UFL, CFL, Premier Lacrosse League, USL Championship.
    • 👩 Women: WNBA, NWSL, England Women's Super League, Women's Champions League, Women's World Cup.
    • 🎓 College: football, men's and women's basketball, baseball, softball, men's and women's hockey, men's and women's volleyball, men's and women's lacrosse, field hockey.
    • 🌍 World soccer: Premier League, EFL Championship, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, Liga MX, Saudi Pro League, Champions League, Europa League, Copa Libertadores, World Cup.
    • 🌏 More of the world: AFL (Aussie rules), Rugby World Cup, FIBA Basketball World Cup, Indian Premier League cricket.
  2. Tap Follow this team.
  3. During the game the score updates every 10 seconds. When your team scores, the wall celebrates in their colors.

Live stats and the Game Center

  • Game Center on your phone: the linescore inning by inning (or by quarter), hits and errors, the leaders, the series and each team's record.
  • Baseball: balls, strikes and outs, runners on base, who's batting and pitching, top or bottom of the inning. Every 16 seconds the wall shows a little diamond with the runners and the count.
  • Football: down and distance, ball on, who has it, and a red-zone alert.
  • The last play scrolls across the wall each time something happens.
GlowBop uses public score feeds and has backups for the major leagues. If a feed is slow, the board catches up on the next update.

F1 live board 🏎️

  • Rows show position and driver code with a team color tab.
  • A tyre dot: red soft, yellow medium, white hard.
  • A flashing DRS attack bar grows under a driver closing inside one second.
  • Purple for the fastest lap holder, a flashing yellow flag stripe for the safety car, red for a red flag, a chequered stripe at the finish, and rain falling on the wall when it's wet.
  • Celebrations in the driver's team colors for NEW LEADER, POLE, TOP TIME, WINNER, FASTEST LAP and SAFETY CAR. Pit stops get a soft flash with the stop time.

Works during practice, qualifying and the race, whenever a session is live.

⛳ Golf, 🏁 Racing, 🎾 Tennis and 🥊 UFC

Tap Live Fan, open the League list and pick ⛳ Golf, 🏁 Racing, 🎾 Tennis or 🥊 UFC at the bottom, then tap Put it on the wall. Its settings (favorite player, series and so on) open right below.

  • Golf: the tournament leaderboard. Under par shows red like on TV, over par blue. Pick a favorite player and they always get a glowing spot on the wall. A new leader sets off a celebration.
  • Racing: NASCAR Cup, O'Reilly and Truck series, and IndyCar. The running order with each car in its own color and the flag on top: green, caution, red, white for the last lap, checkered at the finish.
  • Tennis: ATP and WTA singles. Each match shows both players, the games in every set, the current set glowing yellow and a ball dot by the server. Pick a favorite and they always get a spot; a set or match win sets off a celebration.
  • UFC: the card for the next or current event, red corner over blue. Live fights show the round and clock, finished ones the result (KO, SUB, DEC), and every finish sets off a celebration in the winner's color.

All of them show the next event's start time when nothing is live.

Countdown timer

Pick Countdown timer only as the sport for a giant clock across the whole wall: halftime, a party countdown, New Year's Eve.
