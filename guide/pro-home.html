🏠 Pro Home

House scenes, schedules, zones and the holiday calendar.

House scenesSchedulesZonesHolidaysArrange

Pro Home turns GlowBop into the control center for your house. It comes with Pro. Tap PRO HOME on the rail (in the ★ Pro group) and the page opens with everything you need for everyday lights, not just parties.

House scenes

One tap sets the whole house: Welcome home, Dinner, Party, Movie night, Game day, Relax, Bedtime and All off. Each scene picks a look, a brightness and a pace, and it fades in smoothly.

Schedules

  1. In Schedules, tap Add.
  2. Pick when: sunset, sunrise or a clock time, and which days.
  3. Pick what: turn on, turn off or start a scene.

Your lights come on at sunset and go off at bedtime every day, even when nobody opens the app. The laptop just needs to be on with GlowBop running.

Sunset needs your town. Set your location once on the LIVE page (the weather feed asks for it) so GlowBop knows when sunset is where you live.

Zones

Pro Home brings in your light groups (from LIGHTS), so the front porch, the pool cage and the living room can each run their own show, plus a brightness fader for each zone (from LIVE BOARD).

Holiday calendar

The holiday calendar (from HOLIDAY) sits on the page too, so you can see what's coming up and switch the house into a holiday with one tap.

Make it yours

Every box on the Pro Home page can be moved and resized with ARRANGE. Put the scenes you use most at the top.
