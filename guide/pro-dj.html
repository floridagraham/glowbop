Unlock Pro DJ

  1. Pick a Pro DJ plan on the pricing page. Your key starts with GBPRO-.
  2. In the console, tap SETUP → License and paste the key. Or tap I have a Pro DJ key on any locked Pro card.
  3. The locks come off right away. No reinstall and no new hardware: the same lights and the same laptop.
On Home DJ, SHOWS and OVERRIDE show a small lock on the rail and the Pro cards show what they do. Song Shows can be looked at, but not played or edited. Want to play first? The live demo is the real console on a pretend wall, and the Pro tools work there.

🎬 Song Shows Pro DJ

Build a light show for a song once and it plays the same way every time that song comes on, right on the beat. Anywhere your show is empty, GlowBop keeps reacting live. Tap SHOWS in the Perform group.

The timeline

Pick a song from the list. Every song GlowBop has heard shows up there, with its tempo and how many drops it has. The timeline has a bar ruler, the song's sections in color, the drops marked in pink, and six lanes:

  • Look: which look plays from here.
  • Hits: drops, strobes, flashes, fire bursts, confetti and blackouts.
  • Color: lock one to three colors in place of the look's own.
  • Brightness: a curve you draw, from 0 to 100 percent.
  • Text: words on the wall, like a crowd line.
  • FX: lock one effect for a stretch of the song.

Tap an empty spot in a lane to add a cue there, or tap a lane's name to add one at the playhead. Drag a cue to move it, drag its right edge to stretch it, tap it to change it. Everything snaps to the beat. Pinch or use − + to zoom, and turn on Follow to keep the playhead in view. It saves by itself, and Undo is one tap away.

Auto-draft

Tap ✨ Auto-draft and GlowBop writes a first show from what it already knows about the song: the tempo, the bar grid, the drops it remembers, the mood and the key. Builds lead into the drops, and the biggest drop gets the biggest moment. Then drag, stretch and tweak it until it's yours.

Record your DJ pads into the song

Tap ● Record on the SHOWS page, then play the song and go to DJ. Every pad, build drop, crowd line and look you pick lands on that song's timeline at that moment, snapped to the beat. A red bar on the DJ page shows while you record; tap Stop when you're done.

How it follows the song

A show plays when GlowBop knows where in the song it is:

  • Exact with GlowBop's own player, and with music apps that share their timeline with Windows, like Spotify or Apple Music, when they report it.
  • Kept on the beat for other apps when GlowBop caught the song from its start: it counts from there and locks to the song's remembered bar grid.
  • If it can't tell, the live show runs and the SHOWS page says why.

♫ Back to music under the wall skips the show for the rest of this play. It comes back next time the song plays. To keep a song's show for later without playing it, turn off Play this song's show on its timeline. Song Shows: off turns them all off at once.

🎚️ Fader board Pro DJ

Six live faders sit near the bottom of the DJ page, just above the loop banks. They ride on top of whatever is playing.

  • Master: brightness for the whole wall.
  • Speed: slows the animation down to a quarter, or speeds it up to three times.
  • Strobe: off at the bottom, then locked to the beat as you push up: every beat, 1/2, 1/4 and 1/8.
  • Color: a color wheel that tints the show.
  • White: blends the wall toward white.
  • Trails: leaves glowing trails behind moving light.

Drag anywhere on a fader, up or down. It moves from where it already is, so a stray touch never makes the lights jump. On an iPad, two fingers move two faders at once. Double-tap a fader to reset it, or tap ↺ Reset all.

Hold keys

Under each fader is a key that works while your finger is down: ⬛ Blackout, ⏩ 2X double time, ⚡ Full strobe, 🌈 Rainbow, ⚪ White and ❄️ Freeze.

Every phone, iPad and the laptop show the same fader positions. When a fader is away from normal, a 🎚️ FADERS chip shows in the mode bar; tap it to jump back to the board.

Auto-release safety: while your finger is down, a hold key keeps checking in. If the Wi-Fi drops or the phone locks, the key lets go by itself about a second later, so the wall never gets stuck in a blackout or a strobe.

⛔ Override keys Pro DJ

Tap OVERRIDE in the Perform group. Nine big keys take over the whole wall at once, whatever is playing, and hand it back when you let go.

⬛ Blackout ⚪ White ⚡ Strobe 💡 House 🔴 Red 🔵 Blue 🩷 Pink 🔦 Crowd 🔤 Logo
  • Hold keys are on while your finger is down. Latch keys stay on until you tap them again. House starts as a latch key, the rest as hold keys.
  • Hold several at once and the strongest wins: Blackout, then White, Strobe, Logo, House, the colors and Crowd. Strobe flashes in the color of a held color key.
  • Tap ✏️ Edit keys, then a key, to set hold or latch, its fade (snap, a quarter second or one second), its color and its name.
  • On the laptop, keys 1 to 9 work from any page (not while you're typing) and 0 releases everything.
  • While any override is on, an ⛔ OVERRIDE ACTIVE chip shows in the mode bar on every screen. Tap it, or ✋ Release all, to let them all go.
Held override keys have the same auto-release safety as the fader hold keys: lose the Wi-Fi and they let go by themselves.

☕ Interlude mode Pro DJ

For talking breaks, speeches and band changeovers. Interlude sits under the override keys on OVERRIDE. Tap 🎙️ Start interlude and the wall goes calm and stops following the music, so a mic or silence can't make it twitch.

  • Interlude look: 🌬️ Breathing, 🔤 Words drifting slowly over a soft glow (BE RIGHT BACK, or type your own, like the event name), 🕯️ Warm glow, or 🎨 A look, slowed.
  • Coming back: a plain fade, or a fade with a flash or a drop.
  • Auto-interlude: pick 5, 10, 20 or 45 seconds and silence starts an interlude by itself. When the music comes back, so does the show.
  • The mode bar button turns into 🎵 Back to the show. On the laptop, press I.
  • Song Show cues wait during an interlude, then pick up where the song is.

🔁 Loop banks Pro DJ

A sampler for your lights, at the very bottom of the DJ page: four banks, A to D, with four slots each.

  • Play: tap a slot and it starts on the next bar, looping on the beat over any song. Tap again and it stops at the end of the bar (or set Stop a loop to At once). One loop per bank plays at a time, and the banks play together.
  • Starter loops: eight are ready to go: Four on the floor, Offbeat strobe, Build riser, Drop hits, Color chase, Flash roll, Blackout chops, and Fire and confetti.
  • Record: tap 🔴 Record, pick 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars, then tap a slot. Recording starts on the next bar and catches the hit pads you play (up the page), on a 1/4 or 1/8 beat grid. Record into a playing slot to add one more layer.
  • Edit: tap ✏️ Edit, then a slot, to rename it, change its length and tap hits on or off in a 16-step grid for each bar. You can nudge, copy and clear too.
  • A 🔁 LOOPS chip in the mode bar shows while loops play; one tap stops them all. On the laptop, L stops every loop.
