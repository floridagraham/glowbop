GlowBop Pro DJ vs DJ lighting software

Pro DJ lighting for the lights you already own. DJ lighting apps are built for DMX stage fixtures. GlowBop Pro DJ brings Autoloops, cue lists with GO, MIDI controllers and Song Shows to consumer lights: Twinkly curtains and strings plus Nanoleaf string lights today, with WLED, Govee and Bluetooth curtains built in and in testing. It listens to the audio, so it works with any music source, no plugin needed, and the tightest sync comes from VirtualDJ and MIDI clock.

🎧

Any DJ software

GlowBop listens to what the laptop plays, so Serato, rekordbox, VirtualDJ, Traktor or Spotify all work. VirtualDJ and MIDI clock give the tightest beat.

💡

Lights you already own

No DMX interface, no moving heads. Pro DJ tools for LED curtains and strings, run from a phone or iPad.

🧭

Honest limits

No Ableton Link, Pioneer Pro DJ Link or Denon StageLinQ yet, no DMX fixtures or fog, and shows react live rather than being pre-mapped to song sections.
